# Bulgaria — Retail & Projects Partner Intelligence

**Prepared for:** Cosentino Export Sales — Silestone · Dekton · Sensa
**Scope:** Sofia, Plovdiv, Varna, Burgas (retail) · nationwide >2,000 m² projects (Volume) · Black Sea coast & mountain resorts (Tourism)
**Research method:** Firecrawl (search + scrape), Bulgarian / Russian / English, cross-checked against own websites, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Google Maps and Bulgarian real-estate/construction press
**Dataset:** 93 retail actors · 32 projects actors (19 Volume + 13 Tourism) · 23 named developments — see the companion interactive dashboard (`BG_partner_intelligence/`) for the full, filterable record set and live scoring.

## 1. Segmentation — what each group is actually selling

**Retail (93 actors).** Price positioning skews high-end: 59 of 93 actors (63%) show High price signals, 29 Medium, and only 2 Low — consistent with Cosentino's medium-to-high-end brief. Service breadth splits almost evenly between full-service "Design+Install+Supply" studios (46) and developer/contractor-style "Design+Construction+Development" operators (36), with a small pure-design segment (7). Recurring value propositions across the retail set:
- **Kitchen / Kitchen & Bath studios** lead with imported-hardware and premium-facade messaging (Blum/Hettich hinges, MDF-lacquer fronts, "technical stone"/quartz worktops already offered as an upsell — direct category adjacency for Silestone/Dekton).
- **Premium Materials Showrooms** already sell marble, granite, travertine and onyx as their core business (6 mentions each across the dataset) — the natural first wave of outreach, since they already have the customer conversation and the physical space.
- **Interior Designer / Architecture studios** sell repeat access to premium residential complexes (e.g. 3Arch Design's recurring placements inside SoHome Residential Park and Green District, Sofia) rather than one-off jobs — the value is the relationship with the developer, not the single project.
- **Hybrid** actors are common in Varna and Burgas in particular (7 and 6 respectively) — typically a materials distributor with an in-house design team, or a contractor that is also its own developer.

**Projects — Volume (19 actors).** Dominated by Developer (8) and Contractor (3) roles concentrated in Sofia, reflecting where the largest premium residential towers are being built. A distinctive feature of this market: **Главболгарстрой / Glavbolgarstroy Holding (GBS)** and its regional entities (GBS-Plovdiv, GBS-Invest) appear as general contractor or developer on four of the largest identified projects (Twin Tower, One Park, Rodina Residence in Sofia; Residential Park in Plovdiv) — a single corporate relationship with outsized reach across the whole retail-facing volume channel.

**Projects — Tourism (13 actors).** Developer-led (6) with a strong Hybrid presence (4: developer + hotel operator + contractor combined, e.g. Dinevi Group). The Bansko/Razlog valley is the richest identified cluster (5 linked actors: developer, contractor, two architecture studios, a golf-resort operator), followed by the Sveti Vlas/Sunny Beach coastal cluster. Golden Sands is a notable gap: extensive searching surfaced only resale portals, no verifiable active developer, contractor or architecture firm physically tied to a current project there.

## 3. Positioning read — price × service breadth

Retail actors cluster into three readable bands:
- **High price + full service (Design+Install+Supply)** — the largest single band (~40% of retail), and the best-fit entry point for sample placement with brand-exclusivity terms: kitchen/bath studios and premium showrooms that already sell, install and source materials in one relationship.
- **High price + Design+Construction+Development** — developers and contractor-developers building their own branded product (e.g. SkyHolding, Burgos Stroy, GBS's own developer arms) — better suited to a specification/framework-supply conversation than a simple sample placement.
- **Medium price + full service** — the volume segment (mid-market kitchen/bath chains, regional showrooms) — the aggressive entry-pricing strategy is most persuasive here, since these actors are actively differentiating on value.

Scored against the same transparent model used in the live dashboard (physical exhibition, typology fit, price positioning, service breadth, competitive signal, contact/source evidence), **70 of 93 retail actors and 8 of 32 projects actors land in the High tier**, and there are zero Low-tier actors in either channel — a reflection of the medium-to-high-end filter already applied during research, not grade inflation. This means the differentiator between candidates is less "is this a good account" and more "which one to call first" — hence the curated shortlist in §5, rather than relying on raw score alone.

## 4. Projects channel deep dive — who concentrates the volume

**Volume channel (>2,000 m², Sofia/Plovdiv/Varna/Burgas).** The Главболгарстрой (GBS) group is the single most important relationship to build: general contractor on Twin Tower (52,335 m², Sofia's first "branded residence"), One Park (40,012 m², Sofia), Rodina Residence (862 apartments, Sofia) and Residential Park (1,475 apartments, Plovdiv, via GBS-Plovdiv). Two of those towers (Twin Tower, One Park) already carry an **explicit ventilated composite-panel facade specification** — a live, named opportunity to propose Dekton as a direct upgrade before the facade contractor locks in a supplier. On the architecture side, **Planning EOOD** (Twin Tower) and **STARH** (ICONICA, Plovdiv) are the two studios of record on premium towers with facades still open to specification. **FasPro Ltd**, a Sofia facade technical-design consultancy, is a high-leverage single point of contact: it writes the technical specification for at least 5 named developments simultaneously (ParkView, Byalo Pole 1, New Land District, Arika, Yantra).

**Watch-out:** **VENTA Group EAD** (Sofia/Burgas) is the official Bulgarian distributor of **Techlam by Levantina** — a direct large-format sintered-stone competitor to Dekton — plus Fundermax, Swisspearl and Apavisa, and offers design/engineering consultation alongside supply nationwide. This is the single most important competitive-landscape fact in the projects channel: any facade-specification conversation in Bulgaria should assume VENTA/Techlam is already in the room.

**Tourism channel (Black Sea coast & mountains).** Dinevi Group (Sveti Vlas) is the strongest single account: 35+ years, vertically integrated developer/hotel operator/contractor/marina operator with 40+ delivered holiday complexes and confirmed large-scale physical exposure — a "zero unfinished complexes" reliability narrative that matters for a premium material partner. In the mountains, the Bansko/Razlog cluster is active and under construction across multiple projects (Grand Resort Pamporovo, Belvedere Holiday Club, Redenka Golf & Ski Club/The Grand Redenka), with **Art New Vision** appearing as the architecture studio behind several award-winning resort designs and offering a confirmed direct contact.

## 6. Key risks to validate with the local team before outreach

- **CityStone** operates in all three target retail cities (Sofia, Varna, Burgas, plus a 2,000 m² workshop) and is *already selling and installing Silestone and Dekton* alongside direct competitors (Technistone, Cimstone, GeoSlab, Belenco, Atlasplan). Confirm distributor status before treating it as a new prospect — this is an account-management conversation, not a cold approach.
- **VENTA Group EAD** distributes Techlam by Levantina (a direct Dekton competitor) for facade projects nationwide — factor this into any projects-channel pricing and positioning strategy.
- **Golden Sands** (tourism cluster) has no verifiable active developer/contractor/architecture actor in current sources — do not assume coverage there; a local ground-truth visit or a dedicated follow-up pass is recommended before committing resources to that specific resort.
